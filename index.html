How to parallel park
  1. Signal, then pull up alongside the car in front of the space, about 0.6–1 m away from it, with your rear bumpers lined up.
  2. Reverse slowly. When your rear wheels pass their rear bumper, turn the wheel fully towards the curb.
  3. Keep reversing until your car is at roughly 45° — the projected path should point just behind the rear corner of the space.
  4. Straighten the wheel and back in until your front bumper clears the front car's rear bumper.
  5. Turn the wheel fully away from the curb and keep reversing to swing the front in.
  6. Straighten up, then pull forward to centre yourself. Aim for ≤ 30 cm from the curb.
Controls
  • W/↑ Drive forward · S/↓ Reverse (brakes first if moving forward)
  • A/D Turn steering wheel · Space Brake · hold Shift to creep
  • C or 1–6 Change camera (1 = driver's seat)
  • Driver view: drag the mouse or use IJKL to look · hold Q/E to glance, B to look back · scroll or Z/X to zoom · [/] seat height · V or double-click to recentre · T tilt the passenger mirror down/up
